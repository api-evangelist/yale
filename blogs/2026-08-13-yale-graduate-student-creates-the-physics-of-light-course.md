Wright Lab graduate student Cecily Lowe is starting a physics course for women at the Danbury Federal Correctional Institution through the Yale Prison Education Initiative. Lowe is teaching “The Physics of Light,” a curriculum she built around discovering what light is, how it behaves, and how we see&nbsp;it.
