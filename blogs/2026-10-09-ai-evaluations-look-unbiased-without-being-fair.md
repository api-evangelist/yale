Whether you’re applying for a job, pitching a startup, or hoping to break into the music business, your first evaluator may be an AI system. New research by professor Tristan Botelho and Ph.D. student Qingyang Wang finds that LLMs avoid overt discrimination when evaluating candidates but still rely on racial and gender&nbsp;cues.
