Healthcare is shaped not only in the clinician’s office but also by the policies, systems, and evidence that determine how patients access care. In a Q&amp;A, Yale social epidemiologist David Fink, discusses these interacting pieces, what drives his work, and how research can better reflect the outcomes that matter to&nbsp;patients.
